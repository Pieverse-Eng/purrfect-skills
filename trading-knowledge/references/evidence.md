# Assessing evidence

## Time and dependence

An indicator describes a chosen historical window; changing the bar interval
changes the question. Technical analysis works from prices and trading activity,
so several indicators derived from the same closes are overlapping observations,
not several independent confirmations. [Fidelity: technical analysis](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/trading-investing/technical-analysis/what-is-technical-analysis).

As an analytical safeguard, specify the decision horizon before interpreting a
reading. Compare timestamps and definitions before calling two sources
contradictory. A partial candle can reverse before close. Avoid selecting a
window only because it supports the desired conclusion.

## Claims, sources and uncertainty

A company report, an analyst estimate and social commentary are different kinds
of evidence. Financial statements contain reported amounts; management discussion
adds interpretation, estimates and risks. Preserve reporting period, currency,
consolidation scope and publication date when citing a number.
[SEC: reading a 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/answers/reada10k.htm).

A source's quoted text establishes what it says, not whether every claim is true.
A filing may be corrected, a summary may omit qualifications, and several news
sites may repeat one source. Follow decisive claims to originals when permitted;
record disagreements and missing information. Observation time is not report
period or publication time. Do not silently convert quarterly figures to annual
figures, gross values to net values, or a forecast into a reported result.

## Interpretation and counterexamples

The following are reasoning safeguards, not a scoring model. Correlation or a
coincident announcement is insufficient to establish causation. State a plausible
transmission mechanism and what alternative mechanism would explain the same
observation. Contracts, hedges, regulation, costs and unrelated business segments
may weaken an apparent beneficiary's exposure.

Divergence is a comparison of histories, not a fact recoverable from a single
indicator scalar. Separate missing data from evidence against a hypothesis and
from absence of a useful investment opportunity. Label conditional scenarios;
do not transform model confidence into a win probability or invent precise
returns and position sizes. Consider what observation would change the view,
and stop collecting when additional evidence is unlikely to change the decision.

Conceptual knowledge does not establish current market availability, exact
execution quotes, permission to trade, or user authorization.
