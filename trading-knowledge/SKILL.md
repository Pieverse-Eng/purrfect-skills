# Trading knowledge

Read only the reference relevant to the current question. The main agent chooses
measurements and makes the final judgment; either research role may consult the
same references. This library supplies concepts, not current data, a validated
strategy, required analysis stages, or trading defaults.

- [Strategies](references/strategies.md): trends, breakouts, pullbacks, reversion,
  ranges and events; when their assumptions can fail.
- [Measurements](references/indicators.md): moving averages, RSI, MACD, ATR,
  volume, open interest and funding; data requirements and limitations.
- [Evidence](references/evidence.md): timeframes, dependence, divergence,
  economic transmission and uncertainty.

In scoped research use the corresponding approved IDs `knowledge/strategies`,
`knowledge/indicators` and `knowledge/evidence` with `read_reference`.
Request supported measurements from market research only when useful. Missing
measurements remain gaps. Nothing here requires a proposal, particular entry,
stop distance, position size, or buy/sell action. Execution skills and platform
rules own trading and authorization.
