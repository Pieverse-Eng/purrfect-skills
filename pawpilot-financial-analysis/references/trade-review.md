# Review one trade

## Evidence

Select one authenticated completed Paper cycle or native Live history record.
Read its fills, plan evidence, costs and real price series where available.
Preserve entry/exit times, original protection and revisions. A plan confirmed
after opening is not an original plan. Use actual source IDs, not model-created
chart coordinates. OHLC within a candle cannot prove exact event ordering.

## Method

Separate process from outcome: what the user intended, what actually happened,
what evidence was available at that time, which rules were confirmed, and what
can reasonably be learned. A profitable result need not validate the process;
a loss does not prove a decision was irrational. Later price movement is
hindsight, not evidence that the correct exit was knowable earlier.

Show known PnL and cost components even if final net or R is unavailable; load
funding-and-costs for interpretation. R requires immutable opening risk and a
complete corresponding net result. Missing initial stop is a specific limitation,
not permission to use a later stop. Do not label unrelated fills complete trades.

## Lesson

Propose a concrete decision-process lesson supported by this evidence and mark
it unconfirmed. The user may edit, reject or explicitly save it. Do not turn one
trade into a proven edge or silently save a trading rule. The Coach Skill owns
that confirmation workflow and original card recovery.
